Welcome to My Personal Website

Hello! My name is Glovias Moh-Degai

I am a 21-year-old student at Kenyatta University, pursuing a degree in Education. I am passionate about teaching and dedicated to shaping young minds for a brighter future.

In addition to my studies, I serve as a Sunday School teacher at GOD'S CHOSEN MINISTRY. My faith is an integral part of my life, and I am born again, finding strength and guidance in my beliefs.

I belong to the Luhya community, and I cherish the rich culture and traditions that it brings. My journey in life has been shaped by the values and experiences of my heritage.

At this stage in my life, I am single and focused on my studies and personal growth. I am not currently seeking relationships, as I believe in taking the time to fully develop myself and my ambitions.

Thank you for visiting my website! I look forward to connecting with like-minded individuals and sharing my journey with you.

Cultural Heritage

Being from the Luhya community, I cherish our rich culture and traditions, especially our food! You know you're Luhya when your meal isn't complete without ugali and sukuma wiki. We take our meals seriously—so seriously, in fact, that if you invite a Luhya to your home, you better have enough food, or you might find yourself on the receiving end of a highly creative “missing food” monologue!

In fact, Luhya gatherings are like a culinary Olympics: who can eat the most nyama choma and still have room for dessert? It's a competitive sport and a cherished tradition!
